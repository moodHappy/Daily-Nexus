Haley Maria Stevens is an American politician serving as the U.S. representative from Michigan's 11th congressional district since 2019. Elected as a member of the Democratic Party, Stevens represents most of urbanized Oakland County, including many of Detroit's northern suburbs. She ran for Michigan's Democratic nomination in the 2026 U.S. Senate election, losing to Abdul El-Sayed, and in November 2026 endorsed his Republican opponent, Mike Rogers.
